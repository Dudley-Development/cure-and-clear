How septic transfer dispatch works

In El Paso County, a home served by an on-site wastewater treatment system (OWTS) cannot transfer title until a county-certified inspector’s report supports an Acceptance Document from El Paso County Public Health. We book a certified inspector against your closing date, confirm the report is filed with the county on time, and chase the Acceptance Document until it is in hand.

The county’s Property Sale Acceptance Document application fee is $368 for the initial system on a property (per El Paso County Public Health’s 2025 program updates). That county fee is separate from the inspector’s fee and from our coordination fee, which we quote up front before anything starts. If a system does not pass, the county may issue a conditional acceptance document; we keep the file moving so a closing is not left waiting on paperwork.
