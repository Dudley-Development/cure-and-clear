How a wildfire score appeal works

Under Colorado’s HB25-1182, effective July 1, 2026 for policies issued or renewed on or after that date, your insurer must disclose your wildfire risk score, explain what drove it, and consider documented mitigation. If the score does not reflect mitigation work already done on your property, you can appeal it. We read your letter for free, then — once we’ve confirmed your packet is complete — build the packet your insurer can actually use: before-and-after photos, the scope of work mapped to your score, and a factual transmittal covering the evidence, ready for you to review, sign, and submit inside the window.

We track the insurer’s deadlines from the day they receive your appeal: 10 days to acknowledge it and 30 days to decide. We handle documentation and coordination only. We do not do the tree or mitigation work ourselves, and we cannot promise a score change or a discount. What we can promise is a complete, evidence-backed packet ready for you to submit on time.
