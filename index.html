For property owners

Four kinds of deadline land in Colorado Springs mailboxes. We handle the coordination on all four. The physical work is always done by licensed, insured professionals — our job is the scheduling, the paperwork, and the clock.

Desk 1 · Wildfire

Wildfire score & insurer documentation

Under Colorado’s HB25-1182, in effect since October 1, 2026, insurers must disclose your wildfire risk score, factor in documented mitigation, and answer an appeal within 30 days.

We match you with a mitigation crew for the tree and brush work, then build the packet your insurer actually accepts — before-and-after photos, the scope of work mapped to your score, and the appeal letter — and file it inside the window.

Got a wildfire risk score letter?

Under Colorado’s HB25-1182, your insurer now has to tell you your wildfire risk score, what drove it, and that you can appeal it. If the score does not reflect the mitigation work on your property, we can help you appeal it.

We read your letter, build the photo and scope documentation packet your insurer can actually use, file the appeal, and track the insurer’s deadlines: 10 days for them to acknowledge your appeal and 30 days to decide, counted from the day they receive it.

We handle documentation and coordination only. We do not do the tree or mitigation work ourselves, and we cannot promise a score change or a discount. What we can promise is a complete, evidence-backed appeal filed on time.

The letter review is free. If an appeal makes sense, we quote the packet work as one flat fee up front, before anything starts.

Desk 2 · Septic

Septic transfer inspections

In El Paso County, a home on a septic system cannot close until a county-certified inspector signs an Acceptance Document. Roughly 800 of these sales happen across the county each year, per El Paso County Public Health counts.

We book a certified inspector against your closing date, confirm the report is filed with the county on time, and chase the document until it’s in hand.

Desk 3 · Radon

Radon testing on a deadline

A purchase contract leaves a short window for radon testing, and a missed date can stall the sale. We coordinate a state-licensed radon tester who can hit your inspection deadline and return results in time for the parties to act on them.

Desk 4 · Backflow

Backflow compliance, batched

Colorado Springs Utilities requires annual backflow-preventer testing for commercial and multifamily properties. We track every assembly on your properties, batch the testing with certified testers, and keep the records straight so nothing lapses.
